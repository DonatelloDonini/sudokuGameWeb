The classic sudoku, with a rival.

Puzzles graded by the reasoning they need, not by how many cells are empty. Play alone against the clock, or race a player of your level on the same grid.

Play now Find a rival

Free, no account needed to play. Works on phones, and offline.

Everything a sudoku player wants

Fair difficulty

Every puzzle has a single solution and needs the techniques of its level: singles for easy, up to X-Wings and Swordfish for expert. Never a guess.

Online battles

You're matched with a player of your elo, you both get the same grid at the same time, and the first to solve it wins. You see their progress, not their numbers.

Pencil, hints and undo

Write notes in the cells, see mistakes in red, undo anything. Stuck? A hint fixes your mistake or shows the next number.

Leaderboards

The best time of every player on each difficulty, and the battle ranking. Times are measured by the server, and cheaters can be reported.

Plays offline

A few puzzles of every difficulty are kept on your device: on a plane or in the subway, you can keep playing.

Your pixel avatar

Build your own pixel-art face, from the hair to the hat. Your rivals see it in battles and on the leaderboards.

Making your own sudoku game?

Use our puzzles instead of writing a generator: graded puzzles with their solution, a solver, a difficulty grader and step by step hints, free and without an account.
